The Designer

About Ayesha

Ayesha began her journey in a small design studio, hand-sketching silhouettes that balanced heritage craftsmanship with modern minimalism. Over a decade later, Ayesha Atelier has grown into a house known for its considered tailoring and quiet luxury.

Every collection begins with fabric — sourced responsibly, chosen for how it moves, drapes, and ages. From there, each piece is pattern-cut, fitted, and finished by hand in the atelier, before it ever reaches a client.

"I design for the woman who doesn't need to shout to be noticed. Elegance, to me, is restraint." — Ayesha
